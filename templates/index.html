The rules

Each agent sees its current cell and its eight neighbors. Usually it chooses the cell with the most food; ties are random. It has a 30% × exploration chance of trying a random neighboring cell instead. If it sees no food, it searches a neighboring cell.

Move at most one cell, collect all its food, pay {{ config.MOVEMENT_COST }} if moving, pay {{ config.METABOLIC_COST }} to survive, and age by one. After everyone's turn, patches regrow by {{ config.REGENERATION_RATE }} up to their starting amounts. Death happens at zero resources or age {{ config.MAXIMUM_AGE }}.

Agents can share a cell. Earlier arrivals take the available food first. There is no fighting, sharing, or reproduction yet. Both populations follow the same rules.
