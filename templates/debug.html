The rules

A timestep has three phases. (1) Every agent moves, in a random order; meeting a rival on the way leads to a confrontation. (2) The agents in each cell split that cell's food equally. (3) Every agent eats, pays its costs, ages, may die, and works or plants farms. Then eligible same-population parents may reproduce, unworked farms are abandoned, and food regrows.

Moving: each agent sees its current cell and its eight neighbors. On its own population's farm, an agent that is still saving up (fewer than {{ config.REPRODUCTION_THRESHOLD }} resources) stays to tend it with probability 1 − risk tolerance. Otherwise: if a rival is in view, it ignores rivals with probability = sociability, or else keeps out of every cell on or next to a rival. If a rival farm with food is in view, it is willing to raid it with probability 1 − cooperative tendency, or else leaves rival farms alone. It then chooses the cell with the most food; ties are random. If even that is less than survival + movement cost, it explores with probability = exploration: it jumps two cells into unseen territory, paying the movement cost twice. If it sees no food, it searches a neighboring cell.

Confrontations: when agents from different populations end up in the same or neighboring cells, each one cooperates with probability = cooperative tendency (halved for an agent with no resources) or competes. Both cooperate: nothing happens. One competes: it robs the other of {{ (config.STEAL_FRACTION * 100) | round | int }}% of its resources. Both compete: each fights on with probability = risk tolerance or backs down. One fighting on robs the other; both fighting on each pay {{ config.FIGHT_COST }}, and the winner (richer fighters win more often) robs the loser.

Farms: only fertile land (wild patches) can be farmed. At the end of its turn, an agent with at least {{ config.PLANT_MIN_RESOURCES }} resources plants a farm with probability 1 − risk tolerance, paying {{ config.PLANT_COST }}: on its own cell, or, when working its population's farm, on a fertile neighboring cell. Farms hold up to {{ config.FARM_CAPACITY }} food and grow {{ model.environment.farm_growth }} per timestep; anyone in the owning population may eat from them. Eating from the other population's farm is theft. A farm nobody from its population works for {{ config.FARM_ABANDON_STEPS }} timesteps goes back to wild land.

The active preset starts each agent with {{ model.environment.starting_resources }} resources. Wild patches hold up to {{ model.environment.food_per_patch }} food and regrow {{ model.environment.regeneration }} per timestep. Moving costs {{ model.environment.movement_cost }} per cell, surviving costs {{ model.environment.survival_cost }} per timestep. Death happens at zero resources or age {{ model.environment.maximum_age }}. If both happen on the same turn, the death is counted as starvation.

Encounters mean the same cell (distance 0) or adjacent cells, including diagonals (distance 1). Both populations follow the same rules.

Reproduction requires same-population neighbors, each at least age {{ config.MIN_REPRODUCTION_AGE }}, each holding at least {{ config.REPRODUCTION_THRESHOLD }} resources, and both off cooldown. Each parent contributes {{ config.REPRODUCTION_COST_PER_PARENT }}; the child starts with {{ config.CHILD_STARTING_RESOURCES }} on a randomly chosen parent's cell, at age zero. Each parent can reproduce once per timestep. A birth at timestep t allows the next birth at t + {{ config.REPRODUCTION_COOLDOWN }}. Newborns first act on the next timestep.

For each gene, the child randomly inherits one parent's value. Each inherited gene has a {{ config.MUTATION_RATE * 100 }}% chance of a normal random change (mean zero, standard deviation {{ config.MUTATION_STD }}), kept within [0, 1]. Highest generation means highest ever born; population birth totals are cumulative since Reset.
